# Data publishing policy

HowAICite publishes methods before conclusions and separates public research from private product evidence.

## May be published

- Versioned prompt panels and measurement schemas.
- Aggregate counts with denominators, failure rates and collection dates.
- Reviewed labels and short necessary excerpts when publication rights permit.
- Source URLs already public on the web.
- Anonymized industry summaries that cannot identify a customer.
- Reproducible code that does not reveal proprietary scoring or credentials.

## Must remain private

- API keys, environment files, logs containing secrets and payment data.
- Customer AI answers, reports, tenant identifiers and private URLs.
- Full third-party response archives without redistribution rights.
- Unreviewed recommendation labels.
- Proprietary historical graphs, scoring weights and raw commercial datasets.
- Any result that could be mistaken for a consumer chatbot observation when it came from an API.

## Required disclosure

Every release must state the publisher, target brand, conflict of interest, collection method, market, language, provider surface, model when available, prompt-panel version, observation window, planned cells, completed cells, failures, exclusions and review status.

A missing or failed observation is never converted to a negative result. A retrieved page is never counted as a citation unless the answer attributes or cites it.
